The 2040 Mission

THE NATIONAL & HUMAN IMPERATIVE VIKSIT BHARAT 2047 · ISRO 2040 · UN SDG 4

Why 2040?

Four urgent reasons this transformation cannot wait.

REASON 01

The students are the 2040 workforce

A Class 6 or 7 student at DPS Mathura Road today will be in their mid-20s in 2040. What we learn right now decides whether India's scientific, economic, and diplomatic plans are carried out by people who are truly ready for them.

REASON 02

India has set its own deadlines

Universal quality schooling by 2030 (NEP 2020), Bharatiya Antariksh Station by 2035, an Indian astronaut on the Moon by 2040, and a developed nation by 2047 (Viksit Bharat). A school that stays stuck in the 1990s becomes a national bottleneck.

REASON 03

Learning itself is changing forever

Generative AI will automate routine fact recall and basic code. Schools must teach students how to question, synthesize, and build original things—freeing our brilliant teachers to mentor hearts instead of grading paper.

REASON 04

The campus must carry less

12kg backpacks, paper deforestation, and manual administrative routines cost health, time, and money. A net-zero India by 2070 begins with schools that prove zero-waste, solar-first living is possible today.

EXPLORE THE 4 MISSION STRATEGIES:

From Factory-Era Rows to Multi-Touch Collaboration

In 2040, the traditional wooden bench facing a blackboard is replaced with modular, circular AR tables. Instead of copying notes, students interact with 3D models together—testing physics equations or examining human anatomy in real-time holographic view.

  • Physical Load: Exactly 0.0 kg (Slate snaps magnetically onto desks).
  • Personalization: AI explains difficult math in visual, auditory, or narrative metaphors.
  • Evaluation: Continuous real-time understanding metrics, not high-stress cram exams.
100% Paperless Curriculum

Zero paper deforestation. Zero missed handouts. Instant cloud synchronization for every student.

A Self-Regenerating Micro-Climate in Central Delhi

Located near Mathura Road's busy traffic corridor, DPS can lead Delhi by becoming a net-positive ecological sanctuary. Using biophilic passive cooling, rainwater percolation trenches, and vertical bio-walls, the school purifies millions of cubic meters of air daily.

  • Air Filtration: 3-stage medical-grade H13 HEPA with UV-C air sterilizers.
  • Thermal Comfort: Shaded courtyards drop heat by 8°C without energy-heavy ACs.
  • Water Security: 100% campus rainwater harvesting recharging the local aquifer.
0 Smog Shutdown Days

Students learn continuously in clean, oxygen-rich environments while outside air remains dangerous.

Hands-On Space Telemetry & Bio-Engineering

Tomorrow’s Indian scientists shouldn’t wait until their Master’s degree to touch advanced tools. At DPS 2040, Atal Tinkering Labs evolve into full 6-axis robotics cells, CRISPR bio-simulators, and direct radio tracking feeds from ISRO missions.

  • Robotics: ROS 2 simulation + physical 6-axis robotic arm calibration.
  • Biology: Virtual molecular synthesis and synthetic genetics modeling.
  • Space: Real-time orbital mechanics using live satellite sensor data.
6-Axis Industrial Robot Arms

Teaching high-precision automation and spatial programming directly to 8th–12th graders.

Liberating Teachers to Do What Humans Do Best

We love our teachers: Richa Ma’am, Anuradha Ma’am, Deepti Ma’am, Usha Ma’am, Prableen Ma’am. A smart school never replaces them. By automating attendance, grading, and scheduling, AI gives our teachers 3 extra hours every day to listen, mentor, and inspire.

  • Zero Admin Drudgery: Grading and scheduling handled automatically.
  • Empathetic Mentorship: Teachers focus on building character, confidence, and values.
  • Customized Support: Teacher dashboards alert when a student is stressed or struggling emotionally.
3.5 hrs Daily Time Saved Per Teacher

Reinvested into one-on-one student discussions, science clubs, and creative debate coaching.
