🎯 Why Do We Need This Website? What is the Purpose of This Project?

This project is not just a school assignment—it is a bold student vision linking our daily classroom life at DPS Mathura Road to India's upcoming national milestones.

1. Why a Live Website instead of PowerPoint?

Static slides cannot demonstrate 2D physics gravity, real-time before/after wipe sliders, or interactive blueprints. A website lets teachers and classmates touch and experience 2040 right on screen.

2. The 12kg Backpack Health Crisis

Middle schoolers carry up to 40% of their body weight in textbooks every day, leading to spinal deformities. Our proposal replaces this with a 320g shatterproof AR Slate holding all curriculum.

3. Delhi Smog & Clean-Air Bio-Dome

Every winter, toxic Delhi smog forces schools to shut for weeks. Our solar-powered bio-dome maintains positive-pressure HEPA air (AQI < 20), guaranteeing zero lost school days.

4. Empowering Teachers with AI Automation

AI doesn't replace teachers; it automates routine attendance, grading, and scheduling, giving our mentors 3 extra hours daily for 1-on-1 emotional connection and guidance.
